A cited answer starts by turning a question into a vector and comparing it with the document vectors. The retriever selects passages, and the language model uses those passages to answer with chunk citations. The Q4 result illustrates a limitation: embedding retrieval returned T1 and A2 even though neither passage contained the laser wavelength. Similarity therefore does not establish that the evidence can answer the question. Checking each claim against its cited passage is necessary, and missing information should remain explicitly unresolved. Next, I would test an answerability check after retrieval, comparing questions with known answers against questions whose answers are absent. I would measure whether the system correctly declines unsupported answers without rejecting answerable questions.
